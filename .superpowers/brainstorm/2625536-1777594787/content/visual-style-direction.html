On managing your time

The biggest single win is to prioritize ruthlessly. Most people spend their hours on what is urgent, not on what is important.

Three techniques that work

The Eisenhower Matrix sorts every task by urgent × important — do urgent-and-important now, schedule important-but-not-urgent, delegate the rest. Time blocking assigns each task a calendar slot, defending focus better than open-ended lists. Pomodoro trades 25-minute sprints for 5-minute breaks, smoothing energy across the day.

The trap to avoid

Tools will not fix it; habits will. Pick one technique, run it for two weeks, then stack a second.
