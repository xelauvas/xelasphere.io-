# Portfolio + Positioning Plan (internal only)

## Portfolio structure (non-negotiable)
- Hero (single line only): “Senior Software Engineer — Designing systems that stay up when dependencies fail.”
- Case Study 1: XelaSphere (≤5 bullets covering scope, failures survived, degraded modes, ownership, scale hints).
- Link within Case Study 1: “Deep dive: architecture decisions & failure handling” → points to the detailed `CASE_STUDY_SECTION.md`.
- Case Study 2: Include only if it matches Case Study 1’s depth (real failures, explicit trade-offs, clear ownership); otherwise omit.
- Footer CTA: “Open to remote senior roles.”

## Resume alignment
- Mirror the phrasing from the case study (degraded modes, third-party outages survived, primary owner) so recruiters see consistency between resume and site.
- Include concrete scale anchors (users, deployments, incidents handled) wherever the numbers are truthful.

## Application targeting
- Focus outreach on remote-first teams, product companies, infra-aware startups, and orgs that take on-call seriously.
- Avoid mass applications; this positioning resonates with teams that value ownership and operational maturity.

## Immediate copy updates
- Executive Summary bullet tweaks: “Optimised for resilience, security, and predictable operation on commodity infrastructure.”
- Failure Philosophy: add user-impact bullet to emphasise actionable errors.
- Ownership: “Primary engineer over multiple deployments and iterations.”

## Scale anchors to add
- State concrete figures such as “active users served,” “uploads processed per day,” or “incidents handled,” and pair them with the constraints they were achieved under.

## Messaging guardrails
- Lead with outcomes (“prevented outages,” “kept users unblocked”), not mechanisms.
- Limit deep-dive essays to 2–3 risk decisions; link them from short case studies.
- Keep first-contact materials brief; the deep dive should never be the homepage.
- Never list tools or frameworks unless they are part of a risk decision or consequence.

## Narrative spine
- Reusable positioning sentence (use once per medium): “I specialise in designing and operating production systems where third-party failures, misconfiguration, and incomplete infrastructure are expected — and handled explicitly.”
